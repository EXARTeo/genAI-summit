# Candidate ranker: training data (datasheet)

- **Source:** historical recruiter shortlist decisions for engineering roles, 2019-2024
  (described population: 48,000 applications). The seed job reproduces it synthetically
  (6,000 rows, seed 42); no real applicant data is used.
- **Population:** 38% women; median 7 years of experience; 12% non-EU nationals, 8% other EU.
  Applicants live mostly in Attica and Thessaloniki.
- **Label:** `shortlisted` = the recruiter put the applicant on the shortlist (past human decisions).
- **Ground truth for audits:** `qualified` = at least 3 years of experience, a degree and at least
  half of the required skills (declared by HR).
- **Known biases:** none identified by the provider. Protected attributes were removed from the
  model's features.
- **Measured statistics of the synthetic set:** `generated-stats.yaml` (generated by the seed job).
