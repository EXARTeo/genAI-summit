# Credit scorer: training data (datasheet)

- **Source:** consumer-loan applications and their outcomes, 2018-2024 (described population:
  120,000 applications). The seed job reproduces it synthetically (6,000 rows, seed 7); no real
  applicant data is used.
- **Population:** 46% women; 12% aged 18-25, 28% 26-35, 34% 36-50, 20% 51-65, 6% 66-75.
  Median net monthly income about EUR 1,650.
- **Label:** `repaid` = 1 when the loan was repaid without a 90-day default within 24 months.
  About 73% of loans were repaid.
- **Features:** see `data-dictionary.yaml`. Credit history length depends strongly on age.
- **Known biases:** young applicants have short credit histories ("thin files"); their outcomes are
  harder to predict from bureau data.
- **Measured statistics of the synthetic set:** `generated-stats.yaml` (generated by the seed job).
