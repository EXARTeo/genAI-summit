# SYS-004 Helios Bank Support Chatbot: system specification

Deployer: Helios Bank S.A. (fictional), Digital Banking. Version 1.0, 2026-04.

## Purpose

Answer common retail-banking questions 24/7 on the website and in the app, and hand over to a human
agent when needed. About 30,000 conversations a month.

## Users

Retail customers and website visitors (any age; many older customers use it for online-banking help).

## Workflow

1. The customer opens the chat window; the assistant "Maria" greets them.
2. The chatbot answers from the public help pages.
3. On request, or after two unhelpful answers, the conversation goes to a human agent with the
   transcript.

## Decisions it influences

None directly; it gives information and routes conversations.

## Human oversight

Human agents take over on request; the digital team reviews 50 random transcripts a month.
