# SYS-001 CV Screener: DPIA summary (GDPR Art. 35)

Completed 2025-02-20 by the DPO of Example Recruiting S.A. (fictional).

- **Processing:** CV data of job applicants (identity, contact, education, work history, skills,
  languages, nationality, postcode) to rank applicants for a shortlist.
- **Legal basis:** steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); legitimate interest for
  model training on past applications (Art. 6(1)(f)), with a balancing test on file.
- **Automated decision-making (Art. 22):** not solely automated; a recruiter approves every shortlist.
- **Special categories:** none processed on purpose. Nationality and sex are kept for reporting only.
- **Retention:** applications 12 months after the position closes; training data 5 years.
- **Risks identified:** unauthorised access, excessive retention, inaccurate parsing.
- **Measures:** role-based access in the ATS, encryption at rest, retention job, recruiter review.
- **Residual risk:** low. No prior consultation with the Hellenic DPA needed.

Note: the DPIA does not assess discrimination risks; these were left to the AI risk assessment.
