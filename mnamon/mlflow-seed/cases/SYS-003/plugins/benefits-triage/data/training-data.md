# Benefits triage: training data (datasheet)

- **Source:** applications to the municipality's social services, 2021-2024, with the caseworker's
  urgency decision (described population: 35,000 cases). The seed job reproduces it synthetically
  (4,000 rows, seed 11); no real applicant data is used.
- **Population:** 55% women; 15% non-Greek nationals; median household size 3; 8% with a recognised
  disability; about 30% unemployed, 15% aged 65+ (mostly retired).
- **Label:** `fast_track` = the caseworker marked the case urgent (eligible and at least two children,
  a disability, or income below EUR 200 per person). About 5% of past decisions do not follow this
  pattern (caseworker judgement).
- **Valid ranges:** household size 1-8, income EUR 0-6,000 a month (all training rows are valid).
- **Known biases:** none identified.
- **Measured statistics of the synthetic set:** `generated-stats.yaml` (generated by the seed job).
