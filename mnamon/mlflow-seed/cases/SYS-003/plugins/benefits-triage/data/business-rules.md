# Benefits triage: business rules (as written by the Social Services Unit)

## Valid applications

- A household has **at least 1 and at most 8 members** (larger households apply through the
  regional office).
- Dependents under 18 are fewer than the household size (at least one adult).
- Declared monthly household income is **at least EUR 0** and at most EUR 6,000; it is required.
- Disability means a recognised disability of 67% or more (KEPA certificate).

## Eligibility (statutory rule)

- Income per household member = monthly income / household size.
- Eligible when income per member is **below EUR 550**, or **below EUR 800** with a disability.
- Estimated monthly benefit = (threshold - income per member) x household size x 0.5. It can never
  be negative and never more than EUR 3,000.

## Triage policy

- Urgent (`fast_track`): eligible and (two or more children, or a disability, or income below
  EUR 200 per member).
- Invalid applications must be returned to the applicant with the reason, never silently scored.
