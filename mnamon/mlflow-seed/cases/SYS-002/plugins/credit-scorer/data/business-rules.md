# Credit scorer: business rules (as written by Credit Risk)

## Valid applications

- Applicants are 18 to 75 years old.
- Net monthly income is EUR 300 to 20,000 (verified payslip or tax return).
- Loan amount EUR 500 to 100,000; term 12, 24, 36, 48 or 60 months.
- Years in employment cannot exceed (age - 16).
- Credit history cannot be longer than the time since the 18th birthday.
- Debt-to-income ratio 0 to 1.5; late payments in the last 12 months 0 to 12.

## Credit policy

- Approve when the score is at least 0.5; below 0.5 the officer needs a second signature to approve.
- More income, all else equal, must never lower the score.
- More late payments, all else equal, must never raise the score.
- Sex and age must not be used. Age may only be used where it is a justified risk factor and
  documented (it is not, in this policy).
