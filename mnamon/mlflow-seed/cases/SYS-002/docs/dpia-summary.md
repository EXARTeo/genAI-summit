# SYS-002 Consumer Credit Scorer: DPIA summary (GDPR Art. 35)

Completed 2024-04-10, reviewed 2026-04-02 by the DPO of Aegean Consumer Finance S.A. (fictional).

- **Processing:** application data and credit-bureau data to assess creditworthiness.
- **Legal basis:** pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligation to assess
  creditworthiness (Consumer Credit Directive).
- **Art. 22:** decisions are taken by a credit officer; applicants are told they can ask for human
  review and an explanation of a decline.
- **Special categories:** none. Sex and age band are stored for fairness monitoring only.
- **Retention:** 5 years after the loan ends or the application is declined.
- **Residual risk:** low.
